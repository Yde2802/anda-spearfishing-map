Rules

  • Rest on the surface at least 2× your dive time (more after deep or long dives). The timer buzzes when it's OK.
  • Never hyperventilate before a dive.
  • Buddy check: one up, one down. Watch your buddy for 30 s after they surface. Tell someone on shore when you'll be back.

Rules

  • Personal freedive spearfishing with a hand spear is legal.
  • No take of any kind inside an MPA or sanctuary (RA 10654 Sec 101).
  • Compressor fishing is banned in Pangasinan (Provincial Ordinance 107-2003).
  • Giant clams are protected.

National rules

  • RA 8550 as amended by RA 10654: no take in MPAs/sanctuaries (Sec 101); destructive methods banned (Sec 97); superlights at night banned in municipal waters (Sec 98).
  • Spears are "active gear": each LGU may ban or zone spearfishing in its municipal waters (0–15 km). Ask the municipal agriculture/fisheries office or barangay.
  • NIPAS sites (Tubbataha, Apo Reef, Hundred Islands …) are managed by DENR/PAMB; strict-protection zones are no-take.
  • Pangasinan: Provincial Ordinance 107-2003 bans compressor fishing.
  • Personal breath-hold spearfishing needs no national licence; don't sell the catch.

Emergency

  • 911 national emergency
  • Philippine Coast Guard Command Center: (02) 8527-3877, 0966-217-9610, 0969-217-4123 (coastguard.gov.ph/contact-us)
  • Philippine Red Cross: 143
  • Venomous sting (stonefish/lionfish): hot water as hot as tolerable (~45 °C) for 30–90 min, then hospital.
