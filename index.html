Protected areas

Caniogan MPA (9.8 ha, 2001) has only a point in the database (16.3017, 120.019). Its drawn box is a GUESS: 350 × 280 m (9.8 ha), centred on that point and laid along the outer edge of the reef flat south of the Tondol spit, the way Magsaysay is a rotated rectangle. Stay outside the outer dashed line. “Tondol MPA” duplicates Magsaysay's figures at a wrong spot in Bani, and the Telbang point sits on Cabungan; neither is drawn.

    Food safety

      Rules

      • Personal freedive spearfishing with a hand spear is legal.
      • No take of any kind inside an MPA or sanctuary (RA 10654 Sec 101).
      • Compressor fishing is banned in Pangasinan (Provincial Ordinance 107-2003).
      • Giant clams are protected.
